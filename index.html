▸ HOW TO PLAY STOXELS

WHAT IS A NONOGRAM?

A nonogram is a grid puzzle. Numbers on row and columns tell you how many consecutive filled cells exist. Reveal the full symbol to win!

CONTROLS

Left click — fill a cell
Right click — mark a cell as empty
Hold Left Click and Drag - fill multiple cells
CTRL + MouseWheel — Zoom in & out to scale
Left & Right Arrow Keys — Move the row clue headers towards the left/right
1,2,3,4 - use Class Abilities
Escape - Pause the Game

DIFFICULTY

Easy — low time penalties for mistakes, 50% less Score
Normal — standard time penalties for mistakes
Hard — heavy time penalties for mistakes, 50% more score

GAME MODIFIERS

Time Trial — 50% less time per level, but 20% more Score
Hardcore — one mistake fails the level, but 30% more Score
Ironman — items cannot be used, but 15% more Score
Classless — Class Abilities cannot be used, but 20% more Score
Treeless — Probability Tree nodes have no effect, but 25% more Score
Modifiers can be combined with each other.

ITEMS & INVENTORY

Complete a level's bonus objective to earn a random item. Cursed items are powerful but also have a negative effect.

QUIZ & Probability Gates

Some levels require answering a quiz correctly after finishing a level in order to achieve the bonus objective, and some levels have a Probability Gate that can be unlocked by solving an exercise.

ACHIEVEMENT CODES

Reach score milestones to unlock Moodle Codes. You can see your progress towards the next achievement in the Highscores tab. More score can be earned by replaying levels on higher difficulties or by adding more game modifiers.

CLASSES & CLASS ABILITIES

Completing Ascension - Levels allows you to select one out of three possible classes. Each class has a passive ability and two active abilities, which can heavily alter the way you approach a Stoxel and help in case you get stuck. Abilities can be upgraded by playing more Ascension - Levels.

CONVERGENCE, INFERENCE & PROBABILITY TREE

Completing Convergence - Levels grants Convergence Points, which can be spent on the Probability Tree to customize the way you play the game. Completing Inference - Objectives rewards additional Convergence Points.
