🎛️ Sound Lab

Every sound on this page is generated live from pure math — oscillators, noise and envelopes, scheduled through the Web Audio API. No audio files exist anywhere. This is the same technique I can use to create real SFX and music for Freebuff / Stoxels.

🔊 Browsers only allow audio after a click — press any button. The two music loops below are fully synthesized: percussion, bass, pads and melody all come from code. Click a track button again to stop it.
